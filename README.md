# 📦 Quick Commerce Inventory Analysis

> **Turning inventory data into actionable business insights.**

An end-to-end **data analysis project** using a publicly available **Zepto inventory dataset sourced from Kaggle**.

The project explores inventory availability, stock-out patterns, product value, discounting behaviour, and category-level inventory distribution to understand where inventory issues may impact business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

In quick commerce, product availability directly affects the customer experience and potential revenue.

This analysis focuses on questions such as:

* Which product categories experience the most stock-outs?
* Which unavailable products represent higher potential revenue?
* How is inventory distributed across categories?
* Are heavily discounted products more likely to be unavailable?
* Which products/categories require closer inventory attention?
* What patterns can help improve inventory planning?

## 📌 Dataset

**Source:** Publicly available Zepto inventory dataset from Kaggle.

The dataset contains product-level information such as:

* Category
* Product name
* MRP
* Discount %
* Available quantity
* Discounted selling price
* Weight
* Out-of-stock status
* Quantity

> **Note:** This is a publicly available dataset and is used for analytical and educational purposes. The analysis should not be interpreted as an official representation of Zepto's current inventory or operations.

Good data analysis is not just about finding numbers it's about asking the right business questions and turning those numbers into useful decisions.
